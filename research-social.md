## Highbury Fields weekend mix-ins

- Venue: `highbury-fields`.
- Primary organizer/community source: [Tennis on Highbury Fields — Weekend Mix-Ins](https://tennisonhighburyfields.co.uk/weekend-mix-ins/).
- Volunteer-run social doubles for adults 18+. Arrive without a partner; no advance booking, first come within capacity. Basic serve, forehand and backhand skills are recommended for enjoyment. Tennis balls provided; weather can prevent play.
- **Conflicting published details:** the steering group's page (marked updated 29 April 2024) says Saturday 11am–1pm, Sunday 3pm–5pm, courts 7/8 and £6 cash. The [club's Other Tennis page](https://www.highburytennisclub.com/other-tennis.html) says Sunday 4pm–6pm; its [undated flyer](https://www.highburytennisclub.com/uploads/1/2/9/2/12920550/weekend_drop-in_mix-ins_1.pdf) says courts 5/6 and £5.
- Product treatment: use “Weekend social doubles” / “Weekends · confirm with the organiser”. Saturday 11am–1pm agrees across sources, but do not present Sunday times, court numbers or a price as settled. Link directly to the steering group for joining details and disclose the discrepancy. The mix-ins are distinct from Highbury Tennis Club membership.

## Islington Tennis Centre social tennis, age 50+

- Venue: `islington-tennis-centre`.
- Official operator: [Better — Senior Activities](https://www.better.org.uk/leisure-centre/london/islington/islingtontc/senior-activities).
- Published weekly schedule: Monday 2pm–4pm; Wednesday noon–2pm. Both explicitly age 50+, intermediate/advanced.
- Reception arranges rotating doubles matches. Balls supplied and rackets available to borrow. The page recommends intermediate ability or above. Use the operator's booking route for actual attendance and prices.
- Product treatment: “Social doubles · 50+”, “Mon 14:00–16:00 · Wed 12:00–14:00”, “Published weekly schedule; check with Better before travelling”. Do not conflate the page's separate age-60+ membership offer with the age-50+ tennis session eligibility.

## Local Tennis Leagues

- Venue link: `highbury-fields` as a local anchor; matches are arranged across local public courts, not confined to that venue.
- Primary sources: [Highbury Fields league information](https://clubspark.lta.org.uk/HighburyFieldsLondon/TennisLeagues), [LTA — Barclays Local Tennis Leagues](https://www.lta.org.uk/compete/adult/local-tennis-leagues/).
- Adults 18+. The LTA runs beginner and open options and groups players by standard. Players arrange matches with their opponents. Registration and local round selection now use the official Local Tennis Leagues app; an LTA account and payment may be required.
- Product treatment: “Find an opponent through a league”, “Arrange your own match times”, “Check current Islington/Highbury leagues in the official app”. The older ClubSpark page has dated promotional details; do not repeat its tennis-ball reward or assume the next generic national round is available locally.
- Excluded alternative: [Spin Islington](https://www.spintennisapp.com/leagues/islington) currently says there are no available seasons. Do not advertise open entry or live players there.

## Highbury Tennis Club: returners and club play

- Venue: `highbury-fields`.
- Primary club source: [Highbury Tennis Club — About](https://www.highburytennisclub.com/about.html).
- Sociable club with doubles teams, practice and coaching. Requests a try-out/assessment before joining so it can place a player in an appropriate session. Not a children's or complete-beginner coaching programme.
- A typical Returners & Improvers session is listed Monday 9am–noon. Team sessions have separate ability/availability expectations. Club membership and each session's court/coaching costs are distinct.
- Product treatment: “Returners & club tennis”, “Assessment first”, “Typical Monday morning returners session; confirm with the club”. Do not describe a try-out request as a confirmed membership or a club session as open drop-in access.

## Tennis for All: adult groups at Rosemary Gardens

- Venue: `rosemary-gardens`.
- Primary organizer sources: [Tennis for All](https://clubspark.lta.org.uk/TennisforAll1), [Adult coaching](https://clubspark.lta.org.uk/TennisforAll1/Coaching/Adult).
- Programme includes Rosemary Gardens and welcomes adults at beginner, intermediate and advanced levels. Adult classes aim to help people meet others of a similar standard; equipment is available. The broader programme mixes free and low-cost offerings.
- Exact Rosemary dates and spaces were not verified. A specific [adult intermediate listing](https://clubspark.lta.org.uk/TennisforAll1/Coaching/Summary/3194c3aa-8c7c-43fb-ae1b-4c50b89500a8) has no sessions available to book, so it is not evidence of currently available free tennis.
- Product treatment: “Learn with a group”, “Choose your level”, “See current Rosemary courses with the organiser”. Do not claim every class is free or promise women-only/age-specific sessions without checking a current course.

## Tufnell Park evidence gap

No current organizer-run recurring social tennis group was verified at Tufnell Park in this research. Keep ordinary court booking available and do not invent a social calendar. The [council's leisure-centres page](https://www.islington.gov.uk/physical-activity-parks-and-trees/sport-and-physical-activity/islington-leisure-centres.) confirms an age-60+ court-hire offer with a qualifying Better membership at Tufnell Park, Highbury Fields and Rosemary Gardens on weekdays 8am–4pm; this is a court-hire concession, not a hosted social session.

## Implementation boundaries

Use an external organizer action such as “See joining details”, “View sessions” or “Find a league”. The map can highlight each verified venue while the directory explains ability, age and access requirements. Avoid fabricated profiles, player counts, matching results, “spaces available” badges, automatically generated dated events and RSVP/booking confirmations. A personal shortlist can store only the user's local choices without implying a public community or confirmed attendance.
