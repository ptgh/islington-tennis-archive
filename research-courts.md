# Islington tennis venue research

Research checked 20 September 2026. Sources were inspected online; no site visits or booking transactions were made. This is a sourced starting inventory, not a claim that every unadvertised residential or institutional court has been found. Public booking availability is not live data. A venue listed as bookable means an official booking route exists, not that a slot is available.

## Coverage and counts

- Four public bookable venues in Islington: Highbury Fields, Islington Tennis Centre, Rosemary Gardens and Tufnell Park Playing Fields. Together their published physical inventories total **23 courts: 17 outdoor and 6 indoor**.
- Elizabeth Garrett Anderson School publishes **4 outdoor tennis courts**, with no public tennis hire verified.
- Spa Fields has a **shared tarmac ball court with tennis nets**, free to use without booking. It should be represented separately from dedicated full-size court totals.
- Thus this research supports **six in-borough locations**, with different access conditions. Do not advertise six public booking venues or an exhaustive private-court census.
- Highbury Tennis Club and other coaching/social groups at Highbury Fields share the existing courts and must not inflate physical venue or court counts.
- No separate, current members-only tennis club with its own grounds inside the borough was verified. Nearby private clubs and residents-only courts are explicitly outside Islington.

## Prices and booking copy

The current Better parks price table lists adult non-member one-hour court hire at £12.35 for Highbury Fields, Rosemary Gardens and Tufnell Park, with member and junior concessions: https://www.better.org.uk/leisure-centre/london/islington/islington-parks/prices . ITC's tennis page lists standard adult indoor £40 and outdoor £14.85. These are observed published rates on the research date, not guaranteed checkout quotes. Prefer “Check times & prices” and retain the checked date if prices are shown.

Do not show invented available slots, reservation confirmations, payments or “Book now” for restricted venues. The starter website can support discovery and hand off to the official operator; it has not integrated live inventories.

- **Highbury Fields School / Highbury Grove Academy / Holloway School / Islington Arts and Media School:** council's 2018 audit records halls or artificial pitches, not separate tennis-court inventories for these schools. - **King Square estate, Wilmington Square, St Luke's:** no current authoritative evidence of dedicated tennis courts found in targeted searches. Do not manufacture listings from an estate's multi-use pitch, table-tennis activity or a “court” in its address. - **Estate sessions / PlayZones:** a tennis activity programme does not by itself prove dedicated courts. Council's general PlayZone page names tennis as a possible use of the facility type, not as a confirmed installed feature of every location. https://www.letstalk.islington.gov.uk/playzones

- **Private residential coverage:** no exhaustive public register was verified. Future additions should require owner/manager confirmation or clear current official evidence, an access label, checked date and source. Avoid identifying speculative private-home courts from aerial imagery.
- **Map precision:** the miniature view should say “Illustrative map” until actual borough polygons, surveyed venue entrances and accurate street/transit geometry are loaded. Stylised train tracks are decorative; do not describe them as an exact Underground route.
