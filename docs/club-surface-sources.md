# Club surface texture sources

Photographic PBR texture sets from Poly Haven, licensed CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license (verified 9 October 2026).

- Grass: https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_ground — diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness, 1k JPG.
- Concrete: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_02 — diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness, 1k JPG; selected after visual inspection to avoid heavily stained paint-like patches.
- Synthetic acrylic aggregate: https://polyhaven.com/a/clean_asphalt — diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness, 1k JPG, tinted as court coating.

Maps are hosted through project asset pointers and shared by the clubs and Islington map, including Highbury's separate eleven-court model. The existing mapped surface selects each club finish; grass remains the club fallback for unknown surfaces, and clay is retained. Islington's tarmac, macadam and acrylic listings use the synthetic aggregate finish; unpublished or mixed-surface venues retain their existing illustrative hard courts rather than inventing per-court surface assignments. Subtle wear and mowing are illustrative, not a survey of current court condition. Concrete maintenance edging and diamond fencing are illustrative details, not new booking or venue facts.
