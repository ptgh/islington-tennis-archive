The archive photographs were recovered from the 2022-12-29 capture of Wilson's original `/sites/default/files/` images. The article's generation captions identify the respective models and periods. The existing hashed blog image links are no longer usable. The other 25 current photos are individual Wilson product images; T2000 is from the International Tennis Hall of Fame and SW102 from tennisnuts.
